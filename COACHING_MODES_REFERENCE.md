# 🏌️ Coaching Modes Quick Reference

This guide explains the three AI coaching modes and when to use each one.

## Mode Overview

| Mode | Color | Best For | Response Style |
|------|-------|----------|----------------|
| 🏌️ **General Coach** | Green | Golf advice, strategy, swing tips | Conversational, balanced |
| 🏋️ **Practice Mode** | Orange | Practice plans, drills, skill development | Structured, specific |
| 📊 **SG Analysis** | Blue | Data insights, performance analysis | Analytical, data-driven |

---

## 🏌️ General Coach Mode

### Purpose
Your all-around golf coach for general questions, technique tips, and strategic advice.

### Coaching Style
- **Conversational** - Natural, easy-to-understand language
- **Balanced** - Mixes technical knowledge with practical wisdom
- **Holistic** - Considers all aspects of golf
- **Supportive** - Encouraging while being honest
- **Context-Aware** - References your clubs, stats, and performance

### Best Used For

✅ **Swing Thoughts**
- "What should I think about during my backswing?"
- "How can I improve my driver contact?"
- "Any tips for more consistent ball striking?"

✅ **Course Management**
- "How should I play a dogleg right with trouble left?"
- "When should I lay up vs. go for it?"
- "What's the smart play on a par 5?"

✅ **Mental Game**
- "How do I stay calm under pressure?"
- "What's a good pre-shot routine?"
- "How can I recover after a bad hole?"

✅ **Equipment Questions**
- "Should I add a driving iron?"
- "What loft wedges do I need?"
- "Is my club setup optimal for my game?"

✅ **General Improvement**
- "What should I work on to lower my scores?"
- "How can I break 80/90/100?"
- "Where do beginners struggle most?"

### Example Conversations

**Q:** *"I'm struggling with my driver accuracy. Any advice?"*

**A:** *"Looking at your setup with the Titleist TSR3 at 9°, let's start with some fundamentals. Driver accuracy often comes down to three things:*

*1. **Setup** - Make sure your stance is wide enough for stability. Ball position should be inside your left heel.*

*2. **Swing Path** - For right-handed golfers, the most common miss is a slice. Try feeling like you're swinging more 'in-to-out', like you're hitting the ball to right field in baseball.*

*3. **Grip Pressure** - Tension kills distance and accuracy. Hold the club with a 5/10 grip pressure.*

*Given your handicap index of 12.4, you're already a solid player. I'd recommend filming your swing from down-the-line and face-on to identify your specific pattern. Want to talk about a practice drill to work on this?"*

---

**Q:** *"How should I approach my next round on a tight course?"*

**A:** *"Great question! On tight courses, smart strategy beats heroic shots every time. Here's my advice:*

*1. **Tee Clubs** - Don't automatically pull driver. Look at your 3-wood or even 5-iron off many tees if it keeps you in play.*

*2. **Target Selection** - Aim for the fat part of the fairway, not flags tucked behind trouble. Give yourself bigger margins.*

*3. **Miss Management** - Plan your misses. If water is left, aim right of your intended line so even a slight pull is safe.*

*4. **Short Game Focus** - You'll miss more greens on a tight course. Make sure your chipping and putting are sharp.*

*Based on your SG data, your short game is actually a strength (+0.3), so use that! Play conservatively tee-to-green, then get up and down."*

## 🏋️ Practice Mode

### Purpose
Creates time-efficient, highly structured practice plans with specific drills and measurable goals.

### Coaching Style
- **Structured** - Every practice plan has clear sections
- **Specific** - Exact time allocations and rep counts
- **Measurable** - Success criteria for every drill
- **Progressive** - How to advance as you improve
- **Prioritized** - Focuses on your biggest opportunities

### Best Used For

✅ **Creating Practice Plans**
- "Design a 60-minute practice session for me"
- "What should I work on with 30 minutes at the range?"
- "Create a full short game practice plan"

✅ **Specific Drill Requests**
- "What's a good drill for consistent contact?"
- "How can I practice lag putting?"
- "Give me a bunker practice routine"

✅ **Skill Development**
- "How do I improve my iron accuracy?"
- "What drills will help my chipping?"
- "How can I practice course management?"

✅ **Pre-Round Warm-ups**
- "What's an ideal pre-round routine?"
- "Quick 15-minute warm-up before a round?"
- "How to warm up if I'm running late?"

### Practice Plan Structure

All practice plans include:

**1. Warm-up** (5-10 min)
- Dynamic stretches
- Rhythm swings
- Alignment checks

**2. Technical Work** (20-30 min)
- Specific skill focus
- Controlled environment
- Immediate feedback

**3. Transfer Practice** (15-20 min)
- Simulating course scenarios
- Varied lies and situations
- Less repetitive, more realistic

**4. Competitive Finish** (10-15 min)
- Pressure drills
- Games and challenges
- Track scores/results

### Example Conversations

**Q:** *"Create a 60-minute practice plan focused on my iron play"*

**A:** *"Based on your SG: Approach of -1.5, improving iron play is your #1 scoring opportunity. Here's a 60-minute session designed to dial in your distance control and accuracy:*

### **60-Minute Iron Practice Plan**

**WARM-UP (10 min)**
- 5 min: Dynamic stretches (leg swings, trunk rotations, arm circles)
- 5 min: Rhythm swings with 7-iron (20 swings, focus on smooth tempo)

**ALIGNMENT & CONTACT DRILL (15 min)**
- Setup: Place alignment sticks on ground (target line and ball position)
- Club: 7-iron
- Reps: 30 shots to a 150-yard target
- Success Criteria: 20/30 shots with solid contact (crisp sound, divot after ball)
- Focus: Ball-then-turf contact, not distance

**DISTANCE CONTROL LADDER (20 min)**
- Clubs: 7-iron, 8-iron, 9-iron
- Drill: Hit to exact yardages with each club
  - 7-iron: 10 shots to 150 yards (goal: 7/10 within 15 feet)
  - 8-iron: 10 shots to 135 yards (goal: 7/10 within 15 feet)
  - 9-iron: 10 shots to 120 yards (goal: 7/10 within 15 feet)
- Track Results: Count how many land within target circle

**SIMULATED ON-COURSE (10 min)**
- Hit a random sequence of irons as if playing your home course
- Example: 5-iron, 9-iron, 7-iron, PW, 6-iron, 8-iron
- Pick specific targets for each
- No mulligans - commit to each shot like on the course

**FINISH: COMPETITIVE GAME (5 min)**
- "21 Point Challenge"
- Hit 7 shots with your 7-iron to 150 yards
- Scoring:
  - Pin-high within 30 feet = 3 points
  - Within 30 feet but wrong distance = 2 points
  - Online but over 30 feet = 1 point
- Try to score 15+ points out of 21

**POST-PRACTICE NOTES:**
- Record your distance control results
- When you can hit 20/30 within 15 feet, move to smaller target (12 feet)
- Next session: Focus on a different yardage range (shorter irons like PW, GW)

Let me know how this goes! Track your results so we can adjust next session."*

**Q:** *"What's a good putting practice routine?"*

**A:** *"Here's a comprehensive putting practice session that covers all the key skills:*

### **30-Minute Putting Practice**

**WARM-UP: FEEL & SPEED (5 min)**
- Lag putts from 30-40 feet to center of green
- 10 putts, focus on stopping within 3-foot circle
- Goal: Get a feel for green speed, no technique thoughts

**GATE DRILL - SHORT PUTTS (10 min)**
- Distance: 6 feet
- Setup: Create a gate 1 inch wider than putter head using tees, 6 inches in front of ball
- Reps: 30 putts through the gate
- Success Criteria: 25/30 through gate, 20/30 made
- Focus: Stroke path, not result
- **Progression**: When hitting 25/30 through gate, move to 9 feet

**DISTANCE CONTROL LADDER (8 min)**
- Distances: 10, 20, 30, 40 feet
- 5 putts from each distance
- Success Criteria: All 20 putts within 3 feet of hole
- Focus: Speed control, leaving makeable comeback putts

**CIRCLE DRILL - MID-RANGE (5 min)**
- Place balls in circle around hole at 10 feet (6-8 balls)
- Make as many as possible going around the circle
- Score: Count makes (aim for 4+ out of 8)
- Focus: Reading different breaks, consistent stroke

**PRESSURE FINISH - MUST MAKE (2 min)**
- 3 feet: Make 5 in a row (if you miss, start over)
- This simulates pressure - you MUST make them
- Don't leave until you make 5 consecutive

**Track Your Stats:**
- Gate drill: __/30 through gate, __/30 made
- Distance control: __/20 within 3 feet
- Circle drill: __/8 made
- Pressure drill: __ attempts to get 5 in a row

Repeat this 2-3x per week and your putting will transform!"*

## 📊 Strokes Gained Analysis Mode

### Purpose
Translate your SG data into actionable insights and practice priorities.

### Coaching Style
- **Data-Driven** - Based on actual performance numbers
- **Analytical** - Breaks down what the numbers mean
- **Practical** - Connects data to real improvements
- **Quantified** - Estimates potential score gains
- **Prioritized** - Focuses on highest-leverage areas

### Best Used For

✅ **Performance Analysis**
- "Analyze my recent rounds"
- "What do my SG numbers tell me?"
- "How am I performing vs. scratch?"

✅ **Identifying Weaknesses**
- "Where am I losing the most strokes?"
- "What's my biggest weakness?"
- "Which areas need the most work?"

✅ **Prioritizing Practice**
- "What should I practice based on my data?"
- "Where can I gain strokes fastest?"
- "What's my biggest scoring opportunity?"

✅ **Variance Analysis**
- "Why are my scores so inconsistent?"
- "What causes my bad rounds?"
- "How can I eliminate big numbers?"

✅ **Goal Setting**
- "What do I need to do to break 80?"
- "How can I lower my handicap to single digits?"
- "What improvements would drop my index by 2?"

### Understanding SG Numbers

**Strokes Gained basics:**
- **Zero (0.00)** = Scratch golfer performance
- **Positive (+)** = Better than scratch (tour level)
- **Negative (-)** = Losing strokes to scratch

**Performance indicators:**
- 🟢 **Green** (+0.3 or better) = Strength, maintain it
- 🟡 **Yellow** (-0.3 to +0.3) = Neutral, minor gains available
- 🔴 **Red** (-0.3 or worse) = Opportunity for big improvement

### Example Conversations

**Q:** *"Analyze my recent rounds and tell me where to focus"*

**A:** *"Let's break down your performance data from your last 20 rounds:*

### **Strokes Gained Analysis**

**📊 Overall Index: 12.4**
- Range over last 20 rounds: 10.3 to 14.4 (4.1 spread)
- This variance suggests some inconsistency - we'll address that below

**Category Breakdown:**

**🟢 OFF-THE-TEE: +0.2**
- This is actually a strength! You're driving like an 11-handicap
- Gaining 0.2 strokes per round vs. scratch
- Distance and accuracy are both solid
- **Recommendation**: Maintain, don't overthink it

**🔴 APPROACH: -1.5 (PRIORITY #1)**
- You're losing 1.5 strokes per round on approach shots
- This means ~27 shots per 18 rounds - your biggest opportunity!
- Compared to scratch: You're hitting ~3-4 fewer GIRs per round
- **Impact if improved by 0.75 strokes**: Your index could drop to ~11.5
- **Focus Areas**:
  - Distance control (clubbing and swing)
  - Strike quality (catching it clean)
  - Target selection (aiming at fat part of green)

**🟢 AROUND-THE-GREEN: +0.3**
- Excellent! You're scrambling like a 10-handicap
- Your short game saves you ~0.3 strokes per round
- Chipping and pitching are clear strengths
- **Recommendation**: Keep doing what you're doing here

**🟡 PUTTING: -0.4**
- Slightly below scratch, but not a major leak
- Losing ~7 strokes per 18 rounds on the greens
- Likely more lag putting than short putts (most amateurs)
- **Impact if improved to 0.0**: Drop to ~12.0 index
- **Focus**: Speed control on long putts, leave tap-ins

**🔴 TEE-TO-GREEN: -1.4**
- Everything except putting: losing 1.4 strokes
- Confirms that approach play is the issue
- Your driving is good, short game too - it's the irons

### **Variance Analysis**

Your 4.1-stroke spread in differentials suggests:
- **Good days** (10.3): Everything clicks, especially irons
- **Bad days** (14.4): Iron play falls apart, missing greens badly
- **Consistency work needed**: More predictable iron contact

### **Action Plan - Priority Order**

**1. Iron Practice (60% of time)**
- You could gain 1-1.5 strokes per round here
- Focus: Distance control and GIR percentage
- See Practice Mode for specific drills

**2. Putting (25% of time)**
- Another 0.4 strokes available
- Focus: Lag putting, distance control

**3. Maintain strengths (15% of time)**
- Keep your driving and short game sharp
- Don't neglect what's working

### **Realistic Goals**

- **Short term (3 months)**: Improve approach by 0.5 strokes → 11.9 index
- **Medium term (6 months)**: Improve approach by 0.75, putting by 0.2 → 11.4 index
- **Long term (1 year)**: Improve approach by 1.0, putting by 0.3 → 11.0 index

**Bottom Line:** Your iron play is the golden opportunity. Improve that and you'll easily be an 11-handicap. Want me to create a practice plan in Practice Mode?"*

**Q:** *"Why are my scores so inconsistent?"*

**A:** *"Great question! Let's analyze your variance data:*

### **Variance Analysis - Last 20 Rounds**

**Overall Range: 4.1 strokes** (10.3 to 14.4 differential)
- This is higher than ideal for your skill level
- Scratch golfers typically have 2-3 stroke ranges
- 12-handicaps should aim for 3-3.5 stroke ranges

**Breaking it down by round count:**

**Last 5 rounds:**
- Range: 3.2 (10.8 to 14.0)
- Average: 12.3
- *Recent form shows you're trending better*

**Last 10 rounds:**
- Range: 4.1 (10.3 to 14.4)
- Average: 12.5
- *Still quite a bit of spread*

**Last 20 rounds:**
- Range: 4.1 (10.3 to 14.4)
- Average: 12.4
- *Confirms the pattern*

### **What's Causing the Variance?**

Based on your SG data, I can pinpoint it:

**1. Approach Shot Inconsistency** (Main Culprit)
- Your -1.5 SG: Approach has high variability
- Good days: You're probably -0.5 (hitting greens)
- Bad days: You're probably -2.5 (missing everything)
- **Why**: Likely inconsistent contact with irons
  - Sometimes you flush it, sometimes you don't
  - This causes huge swings in GIRs

**2. Putting Variance**
- Your -0.4 average suggests some rounds you putt great, others poorly
- No one putts consistently - it's the most variable part of golf
- **But**: Less impactful than iron variance

**3. Course Management Decisions**
- On good days, you play smart (use your strengths)
- On bad days, you might be pressing or playing too aggressively
- When irons are off, laying up and using your good short game would help

### **How to Reduce Variance**

**Immediate (Course Management):**
- Play to your strengths (driver, short game)
- When irons are off mid-round, club up and swing easier
- Accept that some days you won't have it - play for bogey golf
- Stop going at tucked pins - aim center green

**Short-term (Technical):**
- Develop a pre-shot routine (reduces mental variance)
- Work on consistent ball-striking fundamentals
- Focus on tempo and rhythm over power

**Long-term (Skill Development):**
- **Priority**: Improve iron contact consistency
  - Lessons to identify swing flaws
  - Alignment and setup work
  - Quality over quantity in practice
- Get your bad shots better (a bad approach that's 40 feet vs. 60 feet)

### **Realistic Expectations**

- **Good round differential**: 10-11 (when irons are on)
- **Average round**: 12-13 (your normal game)
- **Bad round**: 13-14 (when struggling)

Tighten that range to 2.5-3 strokes and you'll play more consistent golf AND lower your index.

**Your goal:** Make your bad days look like your average days, and your good days will take care of themselves.

Want me to switch to Practice Mode and design an iron consistency practice plan?"*

## Switching Between Modes

**In the App:**
1. Tap the colored mode button at the top of the Chat screen
2. Select your desired mode
3. Chat history clears when you switch (to avoid context confusion)
4. Starter questions appear for the new mode

**Tips:**
- Start conversations in the right mode
- Don't hesitate to switch if you need a different perspective
- You can always say "switch to Practice Mode" or "analyze my SG data"
